Personalized tips to reduce your footprint

Chennai / Tamil Nadu specific: Use Chennai Metro (CMRL) and MTC buses — among the lowest emission options per km in India. Consider shifting AC usage to off-peak solar hours. Local millets, lentils and seasonal vegetables have significantly lower embodied carbon than imported or processed foods.

Data Sources & Methodology
Transparent • Verifiable
Transportation factors (kgCO₂e per passenger-km):
• Two-wheeler / Car petrol: 0.09–0.16 (adapted from Our World in Data + India GHG Programme)
• Public bus / metro: ~0.05 (lower due to occupancy)
• Domestic flights: 0.20–0.25 (short-haul higher per km)
Electricity: ~0.78 kgCO₂e/kWh (India average grid mix, 2024–2025 data. Tamil Nadu slightly lower with renewables).
Food: Aggregated from Poore & Nemecek (Science 2018), FAO, and Indian diet studies. Values represent production + supply chain emissions attributable to daily consumption.
Full references available in source code. This calculator prioritizes speed and education over precision. For corporate or regulatory use, use GHG Protocol + verified data.
